# A good question

<!-- block:intro -->
A useful comment tells the writer what you understood and what is still missing. It gives the next draft a clear direction.

<!-- block:picture -->
![A simple illustration of a note with a question mark.](assets/question.svg)

<!-- block:example -->
## Make a vague comment useful

<!-- block:practice -->
Instead of writing “This is unclear,” ask a focused question: “Which step comes first, and how can the reader tell it is complete?”
