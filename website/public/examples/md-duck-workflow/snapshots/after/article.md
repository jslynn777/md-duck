# A clearer product inquiry

<!-- block:intro -->

A useful inquiry gives a supplier enough information to answer your next question. Start with a short message, then read it once from their point of view.

<!-- block:request-heading -->

## Make the request useful

<!-- block:request-details -->

Name the product you want, its size and the quantity you need. Include the delivery city and your preferred arrival date so the supplier can quote against a clear request. If a detail is not confirmed, use a placeholder and ask what options are available.

<!-- block:unknown-heading -->

## Leave unknown details open

<!-- block:unknown-details -->

Use a placeholder when a detail is not confirmed. Ask the supplier to clarify available options instead of inventing specifications or promising a delivery date.

<!-- block:check-heading -->

## Read before sending

<!-- block:check-details -->

Check that the message is specific, that the facts are yours, and that the recipient can see what you want them to answer.
