# Check claims while reading

<!-- block:intro -->

When a sentence makes a claim, ask what supports it. Copy the passage or take a screenshot, then tell your AI whether you need a source, an example or a more cautious explanation.

Read the saved revision again in context. A clearer sentence should still fit the surrounding argument. Check the translation when the meaning changes.
