# Hand off a reviewed article

<!-- block:intro -->

Keep the reviewed article and its images in the local folder. Tell an external publishing tool which version is approved and which website or backend should receive it.

<!-- block:illustration -->

![Original local illustration for this reading example](assets/question.svg)

<!-- block:detail -->

Review the destination draft and confirm publication separately. Afterwards, open the public URL and compare the title, paragraphs and images with the approved files. MD Duck itself does not publish to a website.
