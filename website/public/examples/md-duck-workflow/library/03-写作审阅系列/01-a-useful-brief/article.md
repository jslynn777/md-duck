# Give AI a useful brief

<!-- block:intro -->

Tell your AI who the article is for, what it should help them do and where to save the files. Include your confirmed facts and the questions that still need an answer.

<!-- block:illustration -->

![Original local illustration for this reading example](assets/question.svg)

<!-- block:detail -->

Ask for one folder per article. Keep the source, optional translation and images together. Read the result in MD Duck before requesting more articles in the series.
