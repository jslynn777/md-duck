# Ask about delivery clearly

<!-- block:intro -->

Provide the destination city and preferred arrival date when they are known. Ask the supplier to distinguish production time from transport time and explain what information is still missing.

<!-- block:illustration -->

![Original local illustration for this reading example](assets/morning.svg)

<!-- block:detail -->

Record the answer as an estimate until the parties agree on the conditions. A useful article explains these questions without promising a schedule it cannot verify.
