<!-- block:intro -->

Wired ribbon is the easiest way to keep a bow from collapsing before the guests leave.

<!-- block:width -->

## Width

<!-- block:width-body -->

For a standard wreath, start with 2.5-inch ribbon. A tree-top bow can use 4 inches.

| Place | Width |
| --- | --- |
| Door wreath | 2.5 inch |
| Tree top | 4 inch |
